# Executive Summary

The **Modern Software Quality Engineering Handbook** is the flagship publication of the Modern Software Quality Engineering (MSQE) project.

The handbook is designed to become a comprehensive, vendor-neutral reference for engineers responsible for designing, building, validating, deploying, operating, and continuously improving modern software systems.

Rather than functioning as a traditional testing textbook, the handbook presents **Software Quality Engineering as an engineering discipline** that spans the complete software lifecycle.

The book combines timeless engineering principles with modern technologies and practical implementation guidance to prepare engineers for contemporary software development environments.

---

# Vision

To publish the definitive handbook on Modern Software Quality Engineering that becomes a trusted reference for engineers, educators, students, and technology organizations worldwide.

---

# Educational Philosophy

The handbook is built upon several educational principles.

## Engineering First

Engineering principles always precede tools.

Readers first understand *why*, then *how*.

---

## Learn by Building

Every major concept is reinforced through practical implementation.

Theory without practice is incomplete.

---

## Vendor Neutrality

The handbook focuses on enduring engineering concepts rather than individual commercial products.

Technology examples may change over time, but engineering principles remain.

---

## Systems Thinking

Software quality is presented as a property of complete software systems rather than isolated testing activities.

---

## Continuous Learning

Each chapter prepares readers for increasingly advanced topics while reinforcing previously acquired knowledge.
