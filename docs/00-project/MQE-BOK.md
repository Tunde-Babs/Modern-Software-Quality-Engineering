# Executive Summary

The **Modern Quality Engineering Body of Knowledge (MQE-BOK)** defines the knowledge domains, competencies, principles, and practices that collectively form the discipline of Modern Software Quality Engineering.

Unlike traditional testing syllabi that primarily focus on software verification activities, MQE-BOK adopts a systems engineering perspective where quality is treated as an engineering property designed into every stage of the software lifecycle.

The MQE-BOK serves as the intellectual foundation for the entire Modern Software Quality Engineering ecosystem, including the handbook, laboratories, website, learning pathways, and future certification programs.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of MQE-BOK is to establish a structured, vendor-neutral framework that defines what modern Quality Engineers should know, understand, and be able to apply in contemporary software engineering environments.

---

# Vision

To establish the definitive Body of Knowledge for Modern Software Quality Engineering.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

Modern Software Quality Engineering is founded upon one central principle:

> **Quality is not a testing activity. Quality is an engineered property of software systems.**

Quality is continuously engineered throughout:

- Requirements
- Architecture
- Design
- Development
- Data
- Infrastructure
- Deployment
- Operations
- Artificial Intelligence
- Continuous Improvement

# Competency Levels

MQE-BOK defines three progressive competency levels.

Understand principles and terminology.

## Practitioner

Apply concepts in real engineering environments.

Design, optimise, mentor, and lead engineering initiatives.
