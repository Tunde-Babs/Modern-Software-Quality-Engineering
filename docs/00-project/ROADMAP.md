# Executive Summary

The MSQE Roadmap defines the strategic direction and major milestones for the Modern Software Quality Engineering project.

It provides a phased approach for evolving the project from a GitHub repository into a comprehensive engineering knowledge platform that includes a professional handbook, practical laboratories, companion website, learning resources, and future certification pathways.

The roadmap is intended to guide long-term planning while remaining flexible enough to adapt to emerging technologies and community feedback.

---

# Roadmap Principles

The roadmap follows several guiding principles.

- Build strong foundations before expanding.
- Prioritise quality over speed.
- Release incrementally.
- Maintain vendor neutrality.
- Continuously improve based on feedback.
- Preserve architectural consistency.
- Ensure long-term maintainability.

## Handbook and First Edition Milestones

Each completed handbook part is released as a distinct pre-1.0 milestone in handbook order. The part-completion sequence continues through Part XII at `v0.15.0`; it does not make a whole-book publication claim.

After the Part XII release, `v0.16.0 — First Edition Review` covers whole-book normalization, cross-part consistency review, First Edition technical and editorial gates, practical-asset validation where required, and website/publication validation. `v1.0.0 — First Edition Published` is the approved whole-book First Edition milestone.

Potential risks include:

- Rapid technology evolution
- Scope expansion
- Content maintenance
