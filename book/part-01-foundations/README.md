# Part I – Foundations of Modern Software Quality Engineering

---

## Overview

Part I establishes the fundamental principles of Modern Software Quality Engineering (MSQE). It introduces the evolution of software quality, the transition from traditional Quality Assurance to Quality Engineering, systems thinking, engineering principles, and the modern software delivery lifecycle.

This part provides the conceptual foundation for every subsequent section of the handbook.

---

## Purpose

To develop a modern engineering mindset where quality is viewed as a property engineered into software systems rather than an activity performed after development.

---

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this part, readers will be able to:

- Explain Modern Software Quality Engineering
- Differentiate QA, QC, Testing and QE
- Understand software quality principles
- Apply systems thinking
- Understand modern software delivery
- Describe the Quality Engineer role
