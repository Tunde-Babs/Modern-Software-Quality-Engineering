## Purpose

Translate an aspiration to move from QA into Quality Engineering into a measurable, evidence-based development plan. The plan focuses on capability and contribution, not a required job-title sequence.

## QE Capability Developed

Career-direction setting, deliberate practice, evidence planning, and reflective professional development.

## Expected Outputs

- current role and target capability direction;
- 90-day, 6-month, and 12-month goals;
- applied learning activities and evidence;
- portfolio and interview preparation; and
- named support and review points.

## Portfolio Relevance

The plan itself is usually private. Its planned outputs may become **Portfolio Candidates** when they demonstrate a safe, reviewable contribution. Remove confidential employer, customer, and system information before publishing any resulting artefact.

## Reflection Questions

1. Which existing QA strength can you apply immediately in a broader engineering context?
2. Which capability gap will most improve a real decision or customer outcome?
3. Which goal still describes consumption rather than evidence of applied capability?
4. What support must be agreed with a manager, mentor, or team rather than assumed?

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] Current role, target capability, strengths, gaps, and priority domains are recorded.
- [ ] 90-day, 6-month, and 12-month goals have applied evidence and review points.
- [ ] At least one goal has been rewritten as an observable, evidence-based activity.
- [ ] Portfolio and interview preparation are connected to real capability, not title or certification alone.
