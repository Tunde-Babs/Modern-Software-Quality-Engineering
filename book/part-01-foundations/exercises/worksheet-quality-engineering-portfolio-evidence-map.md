## Purpose

Connect learning activity to demonstrable Quality Engineering capability. The map helps a learner plan and explain evidence; it does not require creating every future technical project now.

## QE Capability Developed

Evidence curation, portfolio planning, reflective learning, and communication of engineering contribution.

## Prerequisites

- Read the Portfolio Strategy section of the [QA to Quality Engineering Transition Framework](../../../docs/00-project/QA_TO_QE_TRANSITION_FRAMEWORK.md).
- Optionally complete the competency self-assessment and personal transition plan.

### 2. Identify future evidence opportunities

Examples of future evidence may include:

- an automation framework improvement;
- an API quality suite;
- SQL validation or a data-integrity check;
- a CI pipeline contribution;
- a performance analysis;
- an observability investigation;
- an AI evaluation project;
- a risk-based quality strategy; or
- a Quality System Map.

Do not create these technical projects as part of this worksheet. Select only the next evidence opportunity that fits your current learning plan and context.

## Expected Outputs

- a map from learning activities to demonstrable capability;
- one or two prioritised future evidence opportunities; and
- concise, interview-ready explanations of selected artefacts.

## Portfolio Relevance

This worksheet is a planning artefact. The evidence it maps may become **Portfolio Candidates** when it can be shared safely and explains context, risk, contribution, result, limitation, and learning. Never publish secrets, customer data, internal identifiers, private source code, or commercially sensitive details.

## Reflection Questions

1. Which artefact best demonstrates engineering reasoning rather than tool familiarity?
2. Which capability claim has the weakest evidence today?
3. What confidential detail must be removed or abstracted before an artefact can be shared?
4. Which small next project would produce the highest-value evidence for your target capability?

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] At least three learning activities or artefacts are mapped to capability.
- [ ] One or two future opportunities are prioritised without starting those projects here.
- [ ] Each selected artefact has an explainable context, risk, contribution, evidence, and limitation.
- [ ] Confidentiality and safe-sharing constraints are recorded.
