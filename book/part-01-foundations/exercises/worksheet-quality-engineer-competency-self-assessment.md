## Purpose

Help a learner identify current evidence, capability gaps, and next actions across the competencies relevant to a QA-to-QE transition. This is an **MSQE educational self-assessment**. It is not an industry certification, employee performance rating, hiring standard, promotion rubric, or maturity model.

## QE Capability Developed

Evidence-based self-assessment, deliberate learning prioritisation, and practical professional-development planning.

## Prerequisites

- Read Chapter 8 and the [QA to Quality Engineering Transition Framework](../../../docs/00-project/QA_TO_QE_TRANSITION_FRAMEWORK.md).
- Review the [Quality Engineer Competency Model](../../../diagrams/chapter-08-quality-engineer-competency-model.md).
- Gather safe examples from your work, learning projects, mentoring, or feedback. Do not include confidential information.

## MSQE Educational Capability Levels

Use the approved MSQE educational levels to describe observable capability, not years of experience or title.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Understands core concepts and vocabulary; can recognise relevant problems and follow an established approach. |
| Practitioner | Applies a capability with guidance in a defined context; can use established practices and explain choices. |
| Engineer | Independently designs and implements appropriate, context-sensitive solutions; states limitations and trade-offs. |
| Advanced / Leadership | Improves systems of work, mentors others, defines strategy, and makes cross-team engineering decisions under uncertainty. |

## Expected Outputs

- a 14-domain evidence-based capability profile;
- three prioritised development actions;
- evidence-production and review dates; and
- feedback that has improved at least one assessment claim.

## Portfolio Relevance

The self-assessment is primarily a private development artefact. The evidence it identifies may become **Portfolio Candidates** when it is refined, safely anonymised, and accompanied by context, risk, contribution, result, and learning.

## Reflection Questions

1. Which claimed strength has the strongest observable evidence?
2. Which gap would most improve your contribution to a current customer or engineering problem?
3. Where are you relying on a tool, title, or course completion rather than applied evidence?
4. What capability should remain broad literacy rather than become a deep specialisation?

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] All fourteen competency areas have a current level, evidence, target, gap, and next action.
- [ ] No capability level is presented as a performance rating or certification result.
- [ ] Three or fewer priorities are selected for the next review period.
- [ ] At least one assessment claim is tested through peer, mentor, or manager feedback.
